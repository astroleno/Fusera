# Liquid Glass SaaS Pattern

## Use When

- The route combines SaaS/AI/developer tooling with `liquid_glass`, `dark_cinematic`, premium hero, waitlist, or dashboard-like floating panels.

## Reuse

- Use glass as a sparse hierarchy device: nav, CTA container, key panel, or metric strip.
- Pair translucent surfaces with strong type and calm spacing.
- Keep one accent color and one material language.
- Let product copy drive the hero; glass is supporting material, not the message.

## Avoid

- Do not put every section in glass cards.
- Do not stack heavy `backdrop-filter` layers across the whole page.
- Do not use fake metrics, customer logos, or claims from prompt references.
- Do not default to purple-blue gradients unless the brand requires it.

## Implementation Notes

- Use subtle borders, background alpha, shadow, and blur together.
- Check contrast both with and without video/image loaded.
- Use static fallback backgrounds behind glass layers.
- Keep CTA hit targets and focus states clear.

## Evidence Required

- Contrast checked over media.
- CTA visible above the fold.
- Claims trace to user brief or are marked placeholder.
